SOURCES — pyGeno Scouter

All data sources used in the construction of this project.
No synthetic data. Every entry is traceable to a primary source.

1. GENOMIC DATA — pyGeno + Ensembl
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

pyGeno — Personalized Genome Library
  Author:  Tariq Daouda, IRIC Montréal
  Paper:   Daouda T. et al. "pyGeno: A Python package for precision medicine
           and proteogenomics." F1000Research, 2016.
  URL:     http://pygeno.iric.ca
  Use:     All genomic coordinates, exon structures, transcript isoforms,
           and protein sequences displayed in the interface.

Ensembl Human Genome GRCh38 (release 78)
  Organization: European Bioinformatics Institute (EMBL-EBI)
  URL:     https://www.ensembl.org
  Use:     Reference genome build underlying all pyGeno queries.
           Chromosome positions, strand orientation, gene boundaries.

2. PHENOTYPE ONTOLOGY — HPO
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Human Phenotype Ontology (HPO)
  Organization: The Jackson Laboratory (JAX), Monarch Initiative
  File used:   hp.obo (release 2026-02-16)
  File used:   phenotype_to_genes.txt
  URL:     https://hpo.jax.org
  Citation:    Köhler S. et al. "The Human Phenotype Ontology in 2021."
               Nucleic Acids Research, 2021. doi:10.1093/nar/gkaa1043
  Use:     HPO term names → HP codes. HP codes → associated gene symbols.
           Powers the HPO search layer for phenotypes outside the curated DB.

6. MOROCCO-SPECIFIC PREVALENCE DATA
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

G6PD Deficiency prevalence in Morocco:
  Labie D. et al. "G6PD deficiency in North Africa."
  Reference for ~5-10% prevalence in Moroccan males.

Beta-Thalassemia in Morocco:
  Hafsia R. et al. "Beta-thalassemia mutations in North Africa."
  Documents IVS-I-110 as the predominant mutation in Morocco.
  Carrier frequency ~3% in some Moroccan regions.

CFTR mutation spectrum in Morocco:
  Ratbi I. et al. "Cystic fibrosis in Morocco: mutation spectrum
  and clinical features." Journal of Cystic Fibrosis, 2008.
  Documents elevated N1303K frequency relative to European populations.

Familial Mediterranean Fever in North Africa:
  Touitou I. et al. "The INFEVERS database."
  Documents MEFV mutation frequencies in Mediterranean/North African populations.
